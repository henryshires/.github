## About Zirous

Founded in 1986, Zirous is a technology solutions provider dedicated to empowering businesses with innovative and data-driven strategies. With expertise spanning multiple disciplines, Zirous delivers tailored solutions through its specialized divisions:
- *Revenue Operations* : Streamlining processes to drive efficiency and maximize profitability.
- *Data and Analytics* : Transforming raw data into actionable insights for smarter decision-making.
- *Experience Services (VAEZR Studio)* : Pushing boundaries with immersive VR, AR, and XR solutions.
- *Governance* : Ensuring compliance, data security, and operational integrity.
- *Development* : Custom software and application development to meet unique business needs.
- *Cloud Operations and Managed IT Services* : Optimizing cloud environments for scalability and performance.
- *Integration* : Seamlessly connecting platforms and systems to enhance functionality.
- *Security Operations* : Proactively protecting organizations with cutting-edge identity and cybersecurity solutions.

Through strategic partnerships with industry leaders like Oracle, Adobe, and SailPoint, Zirous combines innovation and expertise to help businesses achieve their goals.
